# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

**Status:** Placeholder — Phase 1 content to be finalized and committed before searching.

## Inclusion (all must hold)

1. Proposes, evaluates, or surveys methods for reducing computational requirements during LLM fine-tuning or adaptation
2. Reports empirical evaluation on transformer models ≥ 1B parameters, OR is a survey/review of such methods, OR establishes a foundational technique later applied at that scale
3. Peer-reviewed venue, OR arXiv preprint with demonstrable community adoption (operationalize: 50+ citations, OR official implementation in a major library such as Hugging Face PEFT/Transformers, OR acceptance recorded on OpenReview)
4. Written in English
5. Published between 2019-01-01 and 2026-06-30

## Exclusion (any triggers exclusion)

1. Pre-training efficiency only (no fine-tuning component)
2. Inference-time optimization only (no training component)
3. Non-transformer architectures only
4. Duplicate or superseded preprint of an included paper
5. Qualitative claims only; no extractable efficiency or quality measures
6. Blog post, vendor documentation, or non-archival white paper (may be consulted; not counted in corpus)
